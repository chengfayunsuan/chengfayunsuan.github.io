A few rules

Credits are not instant

The advertiser verifies the task first, which usually takes from tens of seconds to a few minutes. If the balance has not moved right away, give it a moment.

can be reversed

If a task is judged invalid — uninstalled too fast, fake details — the provider sends a reversal callback and the reward is taken back.

Network requirements

Users on VPNs, proxies or data-centre networks are blocked by the offerwall itself. That is the provider's rule, not a limit we imposed.

Relationship with partners

Tasks are published by third-party advertisers; we only display them and settle the payout. If a reward is missing, open a ticket with the transaction ID.
