FAQ

FAQ

Why can I not see a single survey?

There are three common reasons: studies are targeted by sample criteria, so accounts with missing or ineligible details never match; this round's quota is already full; or you have already taken that survey. Completing your profile increases the number of surveys you match.

What is the difference between available and frozen?

Available is what you can withdraw or redeem right now; frozen is the amount already requested and under review. On approval it is deducted from frozen; if rejected it returns to available.

Why was my payout rejected?

Common causes are a payout account whose real-name details do not match the registrant, an account with an incomplete profile, or repeated requests in a short window. The amount returns immediately after a rejection, and you can fix the details and resubmit.

How do I level up, and where does experience come from?

Experience only comes from verified surveys, and if a response is invalidated the matching experience is deducted. Levels cannot be adjusted by hand and experience is never sold.

Can other people see my balance?

The public profile shows your XB balance, level, reputation and completed payout history. It never shows your email, IP, sign-in history or payout account details.
