NOTICE

Once a payout is requested, the amount is held until it is approved, then paid out within fifteen business days. Code items are issued after approval; subscriptions are activated manually within two business days, and you are notified by in-site message.

  • Exchange rate 1 XB = 1 CNY, and 100 XD = 1 USD. XB and XD convert at the live USD/CNY rate, so switching currency only changes how the same balance is written — never its value. Withdrawals and gift codes use the same rate and carry no extra fee.
  • Settlement time Codes are issued after review. Payouts are reviewed manually and arrive within fifteen business days, later across public holidays.
  • Hold rules When a payout is requested, the amount moves from available to held. If it is rejected, it goes straight back.
  • Check records Every credit and debit is written to your ledger, so you can check line by line and trace any difference by its reference ID.
