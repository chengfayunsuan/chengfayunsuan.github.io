NOTICE

Once a payout is requested, the amount is held until it is approved, then paid out within seven business days. Gift codes are issued instantly; subscriptions are activated manually within two business days, and you are notified by in-site message.

  • Exchange rate 1 CFB = 1 CNY and 1 CF$ = 1 USD, same number either way. Use the currency switch next to your balance in the top bar. Withdrawals and gift codes use the same rate and carry no extra fee.
  • Settlement time Gift codes are issued instantly. Payouts are reviewed manually and arrive within seven business days, later across public holidays.
  • Hold rules When a payout is requested, the amount moves from available to held. If it is rejected, it goes straight back.
  • Check records Every credit and debit is written to your ledger, so you can check line by line and trace any difference by its reference ID.
