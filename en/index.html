FAQ

Can points actually be cashed out?

Yes. Points represent research fees the client has already paid, not currency the platform issues. The 10 CNY payout minimum exists because below that amount the transfer fee approaches the sum itself, which is uneconomic for both sides. Every point's origin, source survey and payout time is listed in the ledger.

How long does a survey take and what does it pay?

Most surveys take three to fifteen minutes, with rewards set by question count and research difficulty, usually a few CNY up to twenty. Length and reward do not always track each other — some short surveys pay more because the sample is scarce. The list shows expected time and reward for each one.

Why do some surveys show up but cannot be entered?

Clients set the sample criteria: age range, city, occupation, or how often you use a particular product. When a quota is full or you do not match, the survey cannot be entered. That limit comes from the research brief, not from your account status.

What happens if I quit a survey halfway?

Nothing is earned. Surveys settle on completion — quitting voids the sample, and the client does not pay for an incomplete response. Make sure you have time before you start.

What makes a response invalid?

There are three criteria: identical answers to every question, a completion time far shorter than it takes to read the survey, or answers that contradict earlier screening questions. Invalid responses have their points clawed back with the reason recorded. If you disagree, open a ticket from the ledger and a human will review it.

How are response data used?

Clients receive aggregated statistics and anonymous responses only — no phone numbers, emails or real names. We never sell contact details to third parties and never use them for marketing.

Are there other ways to earn besides surveys?

Yes. The Earn page connects to an offerwall where third-party advertisers post tasks: try an app, watch a demo, sign up for a trial. Finish one and it is credited at its face value. These rewards arrive through the provider's server callback, so there is a delay of tens of seconds to a few minutes, and invalid tasks are reversed.

Do I need an invite code to sign up?

No. Registration, the Survey Hall, the ledger, redemptions and payouts are all live. Just pick a username, email and password — no invite code, no fee. When nothing is available, the hall shows an empty state explaining why.
