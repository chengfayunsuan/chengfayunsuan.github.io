Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Chengfa (the site) encounters while you register and take part in research, why we need it, and whether we ever hand it to anyone else. It describes what actually happens, not boilerplate.

1. What this site does

This site is an entry point for market research. You register an account, pick open studies from the survey hall, and accumulate rewards as you complete them; rewards can be withdrawn or redeemed for digital benefits. The surveys themselves are provided and run by third-party research firms (providers). We take care of the gateway, identity and settlement.

2. What we collect

1. Registration and sign-in

  • Email address: your sign-in credential and the only way to recover your account.
  • Username: shown across the site and changeable in settings at any time.
  • Password: only an irreversible hash and a random salt are stored. Nobody can recover the original password, including the site owner.
  • Registration and sign-in records: IP address, network location (carrier and region), browser identifier, last sign-in time. Used to spot abnormal sign-ins and block bulk registration.

2. Taking surveys

  • Which survey you opened, and when.
  • Response content is collected on the provider's own page and never passes through our servers. We only store the fact that you took part, to prevent repeat participation and to reconcile rewards later.

3. Money and redemptions

  • Balance, frozen amount, and every ledger entry with its source.
  • Payout requests: amount, channel and the payout account you entered.
  • Redemption orders: item, amount, and the code or reference issued.

4. Communication and notices

  • Ticket content you submit, messages you send, and notices we send you.

5. Security checks

  • Registration and sign-in use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that you are human. The service processes your IP address and browser signals to decide whether a request comes from an automated program. We only receive a pass or fail verdict and never store the raw verification data.
  • Network checks: when you open a page we look up your exit IP once to see whether it is a proxy, VPN or datacenter network, and cache the result for 24 hours. The lookup goes through the third-party service ip-api.com and sends only the IP address, with no account or behavioural data attached. This check exists to stop proxy users from mass-farming surveys. Providers reject proxy traffic outright, and if we let it through, every user's reward pool pays for it.
  • If you open a survey after being flagged as a proxy or VPN network, the account is banned and loses 8 experience. Bans are visible in settings, and you can appeal with a ticket.

6. Page access logs

  • Opening any page records a visit log: the time, page path, IP address, network location (country, province, city, carrier and network), referring domain, browser identifier, plus the language preference and request-type flags the browser sends along with the request.
  • Logs are used only to count traffic and separate real visits from automated probes. They are visible to the site owner alone, never shared, and never used for ad profiling.
  • Logs are kept for 90 days and deleted automatically. Deletion is permanent.

3. What we use it for

  • So you can sign in and see your own balance and records.
  • So rewards are calculated and paid correctly, and settlement is clean.
  • To detect multi-accounting, scripted farming and fake profiles, protecting both providers and your own reward pool.
  • To contact you and verify details when you submit a ticket or request a payout.

We never use your information for ad targeting, and we do not build profiles to sell.

4. Do we share it with third parties

Yes, but only what is necessary to deliver the service:

  • Survey provider: when you take a survey the provider needs to know you are a distinct, real participant, so they receive a participation ID we generate, the survey ID and the participation time. Your email and password are never shared with them.
  • Payment channels: for a payout we pass the receiving account and the amount to the relevant Alipay, WeChat, PayPal or Payoneer.
  • Infrastructure: the site is hosted on Cloudflare, and data lives in Cloudflare's database and edge network.
  • Legal requirements: provided within the legal scope when a competent authority makes a formal request.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not hand it to third parties unrelated to research.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use only a small amount of necessary storage, with no ad trackers:

  • cf_session: your sign-in credential, HttpOnly, valid for 30 days, revoked on sign-out.
  • cf-currency、cf-cur-style: the currency and unit format you picked.
  • cf-fx: an exchange-rate cache so we do not request it on every page load.
  • cf-me: an account summary cached in the current tab, cleared when the tab closes.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile writes its own verification cookie; see Security checks above.

Apart from the sign-in credential, everything is a display preference that clearing browser data removes without affecting your account.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account information: until you close the account.
  • Ledger, payout and redemption records: kept after closure as financial retention rules require, but detached from your identity.
  • Sign-in records: usually 180 days, then cleaned up automatically.

7. Your rights

  • Access and correction: username, bio and avatar can be changed in account settings at any time.
  • Closing your account: submit a ticket via Contact support stating that you want the account closed. We handle it within 7 business days; afterwards the account cannot sign in and the profile is no longer public.
  • Deleting data: you can ask us to delete non-essential data linked to your account, except financial records we must keep by law.
  • Sign out: click Sign out in the top right at any time and the credential is revoked immediately.

8. Minors

The site is for users aged 18 and over. Market research usually sets age requirements, so please do not register if you are under 18. If we find that an account belongs to a minor, we disable it and delete the related information.

9. Policy changes

If the policy changes materially we post a site notice, send an in-site message, and update the date at the top of the page. Continued use counts as acceptance of the updated version.

10. How to reach us

For any privacy question, closure request or data query, submit a ticket via Contact support, or email admin@chengfa.cc. We reply within 7 business days.
